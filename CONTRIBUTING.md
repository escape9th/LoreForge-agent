## Contribution Guidelines

- Keep provider interfaces small and explicit.
- Add a regression test for behavior changes.
- Keep factual evidence separate from creative proposals.
- Do not add fake sources or pretend a fixture is live research.
- Explain meaningful architectural changes in `docs/`.
